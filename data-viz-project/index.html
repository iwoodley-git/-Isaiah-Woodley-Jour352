Are College Athletes in Danger?

Findings from the NCAA survey on athlete betting harassment

By Isaiah Woodley

April 21, 2026

A new NCAA survey suggests that sports betting harassment is becoming a growing issue for college athletes. The February 2026 study surveyed 163 schools that agreed to participate, focusing on abuse tied to betting outcomes. The findings show that men’s college sports have a higher likelihood of experiencing harassment compared to women’s sports, specifically after betting losses. It also found that more popular sports face higher percentages of betting-related abuse than less popular sports.
